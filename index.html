🌹 Shayari 1 🌹

❤️ Shayari 2 ❤️

Ammi ne kaha tha, 'Beta, nashe se bach ke rehna', Main har nashe se bach kar hi raha. Magar jab tum saamne aayi, ek baat samajh aayi, Mohabbat bhi ek nasha hai, aur main usi mein kho gaya. ✨❤️



🥰 Shayari 3 🥰

Chahe aap mohabbat ka jawab dein ya na dein, Hum aapse hi mohabbat karte rahenge. Har dam, har jahaan, har haal mein, Bas aap hi ko chahte rahenge.❤️



💐 Shayari 4 💐

🎁 Shayari 5 🎁
